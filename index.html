Google Drive setup:
1. Open Google Drive and create a folder called "Holiday Receipts"
2. Paste the folder link below
When adding a receipt: open the folder, upload the photo, tap the three dots, choose "Get link", set to "Anyone with link", copy and paste into the app.
iCloud Drive setup:
1. Open the Files app on your iPhone
2. In iCloud Drive, create a folder called "Holiday Receipts"
3. To share a photo link: long-press the file, tap Share, tap "Copy iCloud Link"
Note: iCloud links require the recipient to have an Apple ID to view. Great for personal backup.
Dropbox setup:
1. Install Dropbox (free) and create a folder called "Holiday Receipts"
2. Paste the folder link below
When adding a receipt: upload the photo to Dropbox, tap the three dots next to it, choose "Share", then "Create link" and copy it.
